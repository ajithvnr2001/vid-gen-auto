# 12_feedback

Source: https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353335?hl=en

Report a problem or send general feedback
You can provide feedback or report a problem with Google Flow in multiple ways. Your feedback helps us improve Google Flow.
Computer AndroidiPhone & iPad
Send general feedback
On your device, go to Google Flow.
At the top right, click More   Send app feedback.
Enter your feedback. To help us better understand the issue, add a screenshot.
At the bottom, click Send.
Report offensive responses generated in Google Flow
On your device, go to Google Flow.
Open an existing project.
Hover over the clip you want to report.
At the top right, click More   Flag output.
Select what’s wrong with the output.
Click Submit.
Report a legal issue
On your device, go to Google Flow.
At the top right, click More   Report legal issue.
Under “Select the Google product where the content you are reporting appears,” click See more products  Other.
Fill out the form.
At the bottom, click Create request.
Report an access issue
Google Flow is not currently available in all countries where Google AI Pro is offered. Please note:
VPNs will not enable access in unsupported regions.
Before purchasing a Google AI Pro subscription, check the list of Google Flow supported regions.
If you're in a supported region, reach out to Google One support.
