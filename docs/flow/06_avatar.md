Create & use your avatar in Google Flow
Securely create an avatar in Google Flow, featuring your own highly accurate visual and audio likeness.
Important: For safety and policy reasons, your avatar data cannot be shared via Google Flow Tools or public project links. Google Flow also runs strict recitation and safety checks on all generated likeness content.
Who can use avatars in Google Flow
Avatar is not available in:
The EEA
The UK
Switzerland
All users outside of these regions can use avatars in Google Flow.
Computer AndroidiPhone & iPad
Create your avatar
You can start to create your avatar on a computer, but you’ll need a mobile device to complete the process.
On your computer, go to Google Flow.
Open an existing project or start a new one.
At the top right, select your profile picture or initial   Create avatar.
Select Get started.
Scan the QR code with your phone or tablet.
Follow the on-screen instructions.
Add your avatar to your prompt
You can add your avatar to any of your projects in Google Flow.
On your computer, go to Google Flow.
Open an existing project or start a new one.
In the prompt box, type @me.
Describe how your avatar should be used as a reference.
Select Generate .
Delete your avatar in Google Flow
On your computer, go to Google Flow.
At the top right, select your profile picture or initial   Manage avatar.
Select Delete avatar.
Under the recording for your avatar, select Delete  Delete.
Learn how to delete your avatar in your Google Account settings.
